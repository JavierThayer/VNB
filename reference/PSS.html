Warrant (well-known): A topological space is metrizable iff it is Hausdorff and its topology is generated by a countable family of pseudometrics. (=>) a metric is a single pseudometric, so {DIST} is a countable (singleton) family generating the same topology, and a metric space is Hausdorff. Standard metrization of a countable gauge / uniform space (Kelley, General Topology, ch. 6; Bourbaki, Topologie Generale IX).

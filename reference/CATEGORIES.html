Auto-generated by (catalog) -- do not edit. 27 default categories (one per structure with a morphism predicate) and 5 declared with declare-category!.

Every declared structure X is a category: its objects are the models of IS-X, its arrows the maps f (one per carrier) with IS-HOM-X(a, b, f), composed as functions. The DEFAULT arrows are generated from the slot list -- each operation and constant preserved, a family pulled back, related points sent to related points -- unless the structure declares its own with declare-hom! (continuous maps for a topological space, isometries for a metric space); a refinement (same-shape-as) takes its parent's arrows between its own models. A SECOND category on the same objects is declared with declare-category!: it carries its own arrow predicate and hom-set, and its three laws -- the identity is an arrow, arrows compose, the hom-set is a set -- are OBLIGATIONS, proven in theorem-library/categories.scm and audited at every load (category-obligations-audit). The status beside each law below is read off the theorem table when this page is written.

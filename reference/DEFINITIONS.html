TRAP worth knowing before citing one: mac unfolds a functoid in a GOAL, but mac-h cannot unfold it in an assumption — def-functoid installs no theorem for it to look up, so mac-h warns unknown theorem/macete and the driver continues with the hypothesis untouched.

## What was proven

**The translation (2.14 at a centre).** `IS-ANALYTIC-AT(f, U, a)` is the notes' Definition 2.11 on an open
set U with `f in fun(U, cc)`: a coefficient function of positive radius and a ball `B(a, r)` inside U on which
f is its sum. The power-series layer, centred at 0 since batch 25, is carried to a centre by
`cps-translate-diff`, `-holomorphic`, `-nth-deriv`; `taylor-coefficients-at` states f^(k)(a) = k! a_k with
f^(k) the k-th NTH-DERIV-ON of f restricted to the ball. Also `analytic-holomorphic-ball`, `analytic-deriv`
(f'(a) = a_1), `analytic-at-intro`, and two general lemmas the tree lacked: `holomorphic-compose` and
`alg-zero-deriv-ball` (a zero derivative on a ball gives a constant).

**The principal logarithm.** `CC-LOG(w)` is the IOTA of the z in the open strip with exp z = w, on the slit
plane `{u in cc : not(Im u = 0 and Re u <= 0)}`; existence is batch 34's `cc-exp-open-strip-onto`,
uniqueness `cc-log-unique`, the characterisation `cc-log-spec` by `iota-d`. Laws: `cc-exp-log`, `cc-log-exp`,
`cc-log-char`, `cc-log-real` (log = LOG on the positive reals, through yesterday's bridge), `cc-log-one`,
`cc-log-polar` (the notes' (40)), `cc-log-scale`, `cc-log-mul` (38-B), `cc-log-in-slit`.

**Lemma 2.20, Prop 2.21, Remark 2.22.** `cc-geometric-alternating` (sum (-1)^k z^k = 1/(1+z)),
`cps-log-series` (the derivative recip(1 + z) on every B(0, r), r < 1), `cps-log-radius-one` (the radius is
exactly 1; the upper half through the new general lemma `cc-series-terms-small`, the terms of a convergent
complex series tend to 0, plus Bernoulli). `log0-is-ln` is Prop 2.21 in the notes' wording (the series at a
real t in (0, 2) equals LOG t, by equal derivatives and the value at 1); `log0-exp` (exp(log0 z) = z on every
B(1, r), r < 1, the notes' differentiation argument); `log0-is-log-on-ball`: log0 IS CC-LOG on B(1, pi/(1+pi)),
the ball on which |log0 z| < pi keeps the value in the strip. Remark 2.22's two identities hold on that ball
(`cc-log-one-plus`, `cc-log-one-minus`); the full unit disc waits for the identity theorem 3.35 (or "a
continuous integer-valued function on a connected set is constant").

**log is holomorphic on the slit plane, with log' = 1/w** (`cc-log-holomorphic`, `cc-log-deriv`). THE ROUTE
DIFFERS FROM THE NOTES and is shorter: at every point a, log x = log a + log(x / a) on the ball B(a, |a| r)
with r = (pi - |Im log a|) / (2 (1 + pi)), where log(x / a) is the power series near 1
(`cc-log-holomorphic-at`, `cc-log-im-near-one`). No sector cover, no numerical bound on pi. The notes'
ingredients are proven as well: `cc-cos-least-zero` (no zero of cos on (0, pi/2), from the least period of
exp(it)), `cc-cos-pos-half` (cos > 0 on (-pi/2, pi/2)), `cc-sin-pos-half` (sin > 0 on (0, pi)),
`cc-cos-nonpos-far`, the rotation laws `cc-log-rotate` / `-rotate-neg` (log(i w) = log w + i pi/2 on the right
half-plane), `cc-log-right-half`, and the bounds `cc-pi-gt-two`, `cc-pi-gt-three` (pi > 3, from cos(3/2) > 0
by six terms of the series). The tree's knowledge of pi is now 3 < pi < 4.

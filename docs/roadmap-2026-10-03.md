## 1. Decisions the user takes before the agents start

1. **Winding number.** The notes define it by the integral of dz/(z - a) along a closed path. The
   tree can state that today (line integrals over roads). Decision: define `WINDING(path, a)` as that
   integral divided by 2 pi i, and prove integrality later (3.4 in the notes), or take the notes'
   order. Recommendation: define by the integral; integrality is a theorem of the arc, not a
   prerequisite of Cauchy's formula on a disc, where the winding number is 1 by direct computation.
2. **Cauchy on a disc.** Via the local primitive (already proven for a disc: `antiderivable-*`) and the
   fundamental theorem of line integrals: zero over every closed road in a disc. That is the route the
   tree is built for; no decision unless the notes insist on convex sets, which cost one more lemma.
3. **The general Cauchy theorem of 3.3.** The notes approximate a path by a polygonal one through a
   chain of discs (a good approximation, Definition 3.10) and carry the integral along the
   approximation. Decision: prove 3.3 in that form, with polygonal roads as the tree's roads already
   are, or stop at the disc / convex form (3.2 plus the local primitive), which is what 3.5 to 3.8 use.
   Recommendation: the disc and convex forms first (they unblock everything after), the general form as
   its own batch after 3.8; it is the one place where the shape of the roads (regulated, Dieudonné)
   meets a topological argument, and it deserves a design note of its own.
4. **Measures take values in RR-STAR** (with `POS-INF`), as the notes do, and countable additivity is a
   series statement over a sequence of disjoint sets. Decision: the integral by simple functions
   (sup over simple functions below f, the notes' definition) and measurable functions by preimages of
   the open sets of RR (or of rays). Recommendation: as the notes; the convergence theorems then follow
   the notes' proofs.
5. **Messages as a free algebra.** The strand-space papers assume an absolutely free algebra of
   messages over atoms (texts, nonces, keys) with pairing and encryption, and the freeness assumptions
   (no equations). The tree has no free-algebra construction. Decision: build messages as the
   inductively defined set of finite trees over the atoms (least set closed under the constructors,
   as NN is closed under succ: a `def-by-nn-recursion` tower of finite stages and their union), which
   gives structural induction and the subterm relation for free; or axiomatise the algebra as a
   structure with freeness laws. Recommendation: build it; the construction is reusable (polynomials,
   monoid algebras, the term algebra of VNB itself) and freeness becomes a theorem, not a stamp.
6. **Gamma (3.9).** Holomorphy of the integral for Re z > 0 needs differentiation under an improper
   integral, or the uniform-limit route the notes take (truncations on [r, R], holomorphic by the
   finite integral, converging uniformly on compacts by estimate (96)). The tree has uniform limits
   of holomorphic functions only once Morera is in. Decision: Gamma after Morera, by the notes' route,
   or deferred to the next month. Recommendation: after Morera, as the last item of the chapter.

## 3. What this costs, and what it displaces

Six to eight proving agents a week at the current rate (two to four hours each, under $20 a day of
workers), one integration a day, the exam at night. The manual stays at its October state except for
the three sections the arcs create (winding number and the integral formula; measures and the
integral; strand spaces), each written when its arc lands. The structure graph gains two or three
species (measure structures already exist; messages and bundles are new).

The risk that is not in the list: 3.3 to 3.8 rest on 3.1 and 3.2 being complete in the FORM the
notes use (paths as roads, the regulated primitive), and any gap found there (a lemma stated over
paths the roads cannot express, a closed road that is not a concatenation the tree can form) stops
the line until a design decision. The two agents of week 1 will find such a gap, if there is one, in
their first day; that is the deliverable to read first.

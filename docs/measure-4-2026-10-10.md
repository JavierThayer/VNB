## Routes and the notes' gaps

* Uniqueness without the monotone class or Dynkin theorem: mu <= lambda* on Borel sets (lambda* is an infimum of
  cover sums, mu is countably subadditive); equality on a Borel set inside [p, q] by applying the inequality to the
  set and to its complement in [p, q], both pairs summing to q - p; then continuity from below.
* Minkowski: the notes divide by I_p(f + g)^(p/q); when I_p(f + g) = +inf this says nothing. Finiteness is proven
  from (u + v)^p <= 2^p (u^p + v^p) (`epow-eplus-le`).
* Hoelder: the infinite case is not mentioned; it holds only once the zero case is taken first. "I_p(f) = 0
  implies f = 0 a.e." is asserted in the notes; proven here by Markov's inequality (`ep-norm-zero-null`).
* Prop. 2.24's proof uses monotonicity of the real integral and the integral over a set, neither stated before it;
  Prop. 2.21's hypothesis f >= 0 a.e. is unnecessary for real f.
* Typos in the notes: the proof of Hoelder is headed "Proof of Proposition 2.18"; its last display has I_p(g)^q
  for I_q(g)^q.

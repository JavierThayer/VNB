## What was proven

**Batch 34 (2.19 and the bridge; `cc-exp-surjective.scm`, 13).** Prop 2.19 is stated as the notes state it,
with the tree's `bijection(X, Y)`: `cc-exp-bijection-strip` (the lambda `z in S. exp z` is in `bijection(S,
cc minus {0})` for the SEP `S = {-pi <= Im < pi}`) and `cc-exp-bijection-open-strip` (the open strip onto `cc
minus (-inf, 0]`). Surjectivity through the polar form: `cc-circle-arg` (every point of the unit circle is
`(cos th, sin th)` with `-pi <= th < pi`, by the IVT on cos over `[0, pi]` and the parity of cos and sin),
`cc-polar-form`, `cc-exp-real-surjective-pos`. THE BRIDGE `cc-exp-real-is-r-exp`: for real `t`, `cc-exp(t) =
r-exp(t)`, by the derivative of `log(exp t) - t` being zero (`deriv-chain`, `log-deriv`,
`deriv-zero-implies-constant`, `r-exp-char`); no product rule, no derivative of `r-exp`. The new general lemma
`cc-diff-on-restrict-rr`: a function differentiable on CC at a real point with a real derivative and real on RR
has that derivative as a real function (`is-diff-at`); one page, through the Caratheodory factor. With the
bridge, the positive reals are reached through `log`, so the brief's unboundedness lemma was not needed.

**Batch 35 (the exceptional point; `goursat-exceptional.scm`, 18).** Props 3.6 to 3.9 in the notes' form:
`goursat-triangle-except`, `cauchy-convex-segments-except`, `cauchy-convex-primitive-except`,
`cauchy-convex-closed-except`, under E(u, f, x) = u open, `f in fun(u, cc)`, f continuous at every point of u,
`x in u`, `holomorphic-on(difference(u, singleton(x)), restrict(f, difference(u, singleton(x))))` (HOLOMORPHIC-ON
types its function on exactly its set, so the restriction is the honest spelling). The route: `tri-int-fan`
(the triangle through an interior point is the sum of three triangles with x as a vertex: pure cancellation
of segment integrals), the vertex case `goursat-vertex` as Rudin 10.13 (shrink towards the vertex,
`tri-int-vertex-split`; the two remaining triangles avoid x by affine independence, `cc-affinely-independent`
and `conv3-avoids-vertex`, in real coordinates obtained by skolemising `cc-generated-by-rr`; the collinear
triangle by `collinear-cases` and `tri-int-collinear`), and `tri-int-restrict` for the case x off the hull.
Prop 3.8 needed the brick `cc-diff-on-of-eps-bound` (an eps-delta bound on the remainder gives
`is-diff-on`; the exact converse of `caratheodory-remainder-small`): the derivative of the primitive comes
from the continuity of f alone, as in the notes. `cc-diff-on-local-quadratic` of batch 30 is now an instance of
it (not retired: `goursat.scm` cites it).

**Batch 36 (`NTH-DERIV-ON`; `nth-deriv.scm`, `nth-deriv-laws.scm`, 8).** `NTH-DERIV-ON(K, U, f, k)` by
`def-by-nn-recursion` with the parameters K, U, f, function-valued: zero is f, succ is `vnb-lambda(z in U,
DERIV-ON(K, U, previous, z))`; the recursion helper installs the two unfolds as definitional theorems
(`nth-deriv-on-zero`, `nth-deriv-on-succ`). Laws over any normed field with small elements:

`nth-deriv-on-succ-eq`, `nth-deriv-on-in-fun`. Over CC: `cps-nth-deriv` (the k-th derivative of the sum
function IS the sum of the k-th derived series, as functions), and 2.14 LITERALLY: `taylor-coefficients`
(`f^(k)(0) = k! a_k` on every `B(0, r)`, `r <` radius), `taylor-coefficient-formula`,
`nth-deriv-on-holomorphic-ball`. The definition sits after `structure-library/ordinals` (where
`def-by-nn-recursion` lives), not after `diff-on` as the brief guessed. The real-interval twin already exists
(`NTH-DERIV` over `DERIV`, `higher-derivatives.scm`).

## What was found wrong or stale

1. **THE HIDDEN-ORACLE DEFECT (found by `zero-it`'s check 9b, FIXED).** An oracle (`crs`, `ineq`, `arith`,
   `sos`) fired inside a recording-suppressed step reached neither the proof's oracle inventory
   (`record-proof-debt!`, proof-debt.scm:206) nor the certificate's `own-oracles` (certificates.scm:311): both
   read the recorded script only. Every `bc*` handler that runs an oracle was affected: a proof whose only
   oracle use is inside a handler read `proven modulo 0` with no oracle listed. Both sites now read the
   script plus `*proof-hidden-citations*`. The exam rewrites every certificate, so the stored `own-oracles`
   are refreshed.
2. **The checker's definition memo can go stale** (`*rkw--def-memo*`, rule-checkers-rewrite.scm): accessor,
   functoid and projection rules are memoised under `#f`, so a rule REDEFINED in the same image (an
   extend-band reload) is checked against the OLD rule. Latent: no reload has redefined one yet. The
   certificate hash avoids the memo; the checker does not. Not fixed (a checker file).
3. **`expressions.scm` and `sequents.scm` are not in the kernel hash** although the checkers depend on them.
   Not changed: the user's call (open decision 2).
4. **The what-now structural block silently drops later lanes** (suggest.scm): it replaces the flat move list,
   so the counterexample and user-rules moves vanish whenever it fires; the `zero-it` lane was placed after it
   for that reason. Not fixed.
5. **The brief's slot for `nth-deriv.scm` was wrong** (after `diff-on`; `def-by-nn-recursion` lives in
   `ordinals`); window.py caught it.
6. **Owed definedness leaves fail silently at `qed`** ("proof is not complete", far from the step): batch 35
   lost probes to `fact 'log-in-rr` / `is-continuous-at` at an untyped term. A kit reporter of newly posted
   owed leaves is the cure (open item).
7. **Two `dk-` defects**: `dk-set-close!` cannot close `(IN (CCINT a c) SET)` (three facts do it);
   `dk-split-all!` on a list errors "landed no assumption" when every conjunct is already in context;
   `dk-lam-t!` failed where `dk-lam-type!` worked (again). And the `let` evaluation-order rule extends to a
   `dk-pick` beside a recording call: `let*`.
8. **Missing general laws found**: continuity of a sum in the subspace form (only the constant case was
   proven, `cc-continuous-at-add-const`); `subset-antisym` (two extensionality passes replace it);
   `rfl` does not certify a sum of typed atoms in RR or CC.

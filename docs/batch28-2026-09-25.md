## What was built

`certificates.scm` (a root file, loaded after `proof-debt`) and edits to `load.scm`, `interactive.scm`
(`sp`, `qed--guarded`), `proof-debt.scm`, `macetes.scm`, `prover`, `vnb-band`, `vnb-test`. No
theorem-library or structure-library file changed.

* **The store**: `certificates/<file>.cert`, one per theorem-library file, one record per `qed`, written
  at the end of the file's load when the load is of the canonical tree. A record: name, statement (with
  counter-minted names relabelled) and its hash, the cited names with their statement hashes, `defs` (the
  theorems and functoids the statement mentions, with hashes), bill, oracles, kernel hash, date, host.
* **Validity**: the statement at `sp` hashes to the record; every cited name and every `defs` entry is
  installed with the recorded hash; the kernel hash (the eight files that may call `dg-apply-rule!` plus
  the four checker files; the list is duplicated from audit.scm and checked against it at the end of the
  load) matches. The bill is RECOMPUTED from the recorded citations and any difference from the recorded
  bill is printed, so a citation that went from proven to asserted since the exam is visible.
* **The switch** `VNB_CERTIFIED=on | off | strict`: `on` (the default of `./prover` and of the stale-band
  start) installs certified theorems and re-proves the rest, certifying them; `off` (the exam:
  `prover --build-band`, `vnb-band --build`, `vnb-test`) re-proves everything and rewrites the store;
  `strict` re-proves nothing and exits non-zero listing the uncovered theorems. The 40 proofs that live in
  structure-library files run in every mode.
* **The skip**: in `on` / `strict` the loader reads a proof file form by form; `sp` on a valid record
  installs the theorem (provenance `certified`) and enters the skip state; procedure bodies are REWRITTEN so
  the steps between an opening `sp` (or an opening helper) and its closing `qed` are skipped, a finished
  placeholder proof stands in `*ps*` so helpers that test `proof-done?` reach their `qed` and `topic!`, and a
  "closed" state after the skipped `qed` swallows errors until the next `sp`. End of file: every record's
  name installed, else the file is retracted and reloaded in `off` mode with a warning. The final tree has
  NO fallback file.
* **What the bill says**: `;; qed NAME: certified (exam 2026-09-24) modulo 0`; the load summary prints
  `N proven in this session, M certified (exam of DATE on HOST)`. `certified` counts as `proven` wherever
  the ledger asks. The page audit covers the proofs that ran (a certified theorem has no script here).
* `VNB_LOAD_LIMIT=N` loads the first N proof files, for development.

## Where the design note was wrong

Escaping the top-level form at `sp` lost theorems and metadata in files whose `qed` sits inside a loop or
a helper (`zz-ring-is-ring`, `cauchy-criterion-left/right`, 224 lost `topic!` calls at first): hence the
body rewrite, the placeholder proof and the "closed" state. "An `sp` in skip state is an error" was wrong:
a new `sp` ends the skip state. "Bill from the record" would hide a demotion: recomputed instead. Citations
alone miss definitions: the `defs` field. View specialisation is not a step of `qed`: those companions come
from top-level forms that run anyway. The statement hash must relabel counter-minted names (a certified
load does not advance the counter), and the load pathname must be what `load` reports (the registries
record it; the band compare caught this).

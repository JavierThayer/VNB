   `ascoli-arzela-sequential` lacks the same guard (its conclusion survives on the empty space, but
   no separability route reaches it). OPEN, user.
2. **`BIG-INTERSECTION` is uninterpreted**: two occurrences in the tree (`HAS-FIP`,
   `compact-iff-fip`), no membership law, not a binder to `wff.scm`; and both formulas bind the
   registered accessor `CARR`, which the fatal `constant-binder-audit` does not see because
   `wff-constant-binders` has no case for that head. Nothing proven depends on it. OPEN, user.
3. **The wall in front of Ascoli and Heine-Borel was not only the metric subspace**: there was no
   Bolzano-Weierstrass. With the grid cover the Ascoli route needs no subspace at all.
4. **Stale blocker notes, again**: `completion-is-complete` never needed recursive choice (its real
   blocker was `completion-is-metric-space`, proven 2026-09-18; what remains is about 500 lines and
   one missing brick, "a bound on the limit of `d(f_p, g_p)` reads back onto the tail");
   `prod-of-sums-expansion` has every brick it waited on (a 300-line capstone remains);
   `well-ordering-principle` is not provable against the axiomatised CARD and belongs with the
   pending `CARD := CARD-STAR` decision.
5. **Seventeen groups of PROVEN theorems share a statement under two or three names**
   (`ball-unfold` / `ball-sep-unfold`, `power-in-rr` / `power-real-closed`, three `tgd-` re-proofs
   inside `taylor-proof.scm`, ...). A warn-only `proven-duplicate-audit` would hold the line.

   `vector-taylor-proof.scm`, so the metric laws of a normed vector space cannot join their home.
   Same disease as NPE / GOOD-SUB / LINE (batch 7).
7. **Focus drift by hash-consing** (three sightings: `dk-only!`, `slot`): a tactic that rewrites or
   weakens can close its leaf by landing on a node already proven; focus moves and the next `ass`
   fires on a sibling. One helper, `dk-close-if!`, would cover the class. OWED.

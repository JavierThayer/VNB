\maketitle

\noindent
Every definition, axiom, assumption and theorem the VNB library installs, as a
statement, without proofs.  The first parts follow the user's notes, one part per
document, in the order of \texttt{docs/major-theorems-table.sexp}: its chapters and
sections, and in each section the \emph{major results} (the numbered results of the
notes that the table maps to the library) in the notes' order.  A major result prints
the notes' statement, the major results its proof reaches (\emph{Uses}), the library
theorems that state it, and the lemmas used by it alone, each indented under the lemma
it serves (its immediate dominator in the citation graph).  A lemma used by several
results of one section, chapter or document is printed once, as a \emph{tool}, after
that section, chapter or document.  A result of the notes with no theorem of its own
prints one line saying where its theorems are, or that the library lacks it.

\noindent
The last parts are the libraries of \texttt{libraries.sexp}: the definitions, axioms
and assumptions of each, by source file in load order, then the theorems the notes'
outline does not place -- those used by results of several documents, those of a
library below the result that uses them, and those no major result reaches -- grouped
by the principal defined symbol of the statement, in load order within a group.
A \textbf{Proposition} is proven; its proof is \texttt{(display-proof 'NAME "file.pdf")}
in the prover.

\noindent
A \textbf{Definition} of a structure (a field, a metric space, a measure space)
transcribes its \texttt{declare-structure} declaration (manual, section
``Mathematical structures''): the components, numbered as in the declaration, each a
projection with its typing, then the laws.

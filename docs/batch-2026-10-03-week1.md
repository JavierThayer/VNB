**CA-2, from the integral formula to analyticity** (theorem-library/analytic-from-integral.scm; 26

## 3. The gaps (what the notes say that the tree cannot yet say)

Complex analysis, from CA-1 and CA-2:
1. **3.3, the general Cauchy theorem, cannot be stated**: no n-segment polygonal road (only
   `SEG-PATH` and `TRI-ROAD`); no homotopy (maps on [0,1] x [a,b] are not set up); no
   `IS-CONNECTED`, so "simply connected" (3.19) and the constancy of the winding number on components
   (3.27, 3.30) have no statement. Decision 3 of the roadmap already made 3.3 its own later batch
   with a design note; this is its content: polygonal roads, connectedness, homotopy.
2. **"Positively oriented" does not exist** (3.32).
3. **A closed road has no name**: closedness is the hypothesis `pgam(b) = pgam(a)`; concatenation
   is `JUXTA` on adjacent intervals, with only the affine reparametrisation.
4. **Holomorphy on a subset** is written `HOLOMORPHIC-ON(V, RESTRICT(f, V))`; `cauchy-disc` asks
   for the open disc inside U, not its closure; the circle is the full circle on [0, 2 pi].
5. **The composition CA-1 x CA-2** is one citation IF the integrand terms agree
   (`VNB-LAMBDA(w, u, phi(w) * recip(w - z))` over a set u containing the trace); the coefficients are
   written with a power of the reciprocal, not a quotient; the constant 1/(2 pi i) is a parameter
   `kap`, and |1/(2 pi i)| = 1/(2 pi) with the circle's length 2 pi r are CA-1's circle facts not yet
   proven. The composition theorem (f holomorphic on U, the closed disc in U, then f is its power
   series on the open disc: Theorem 3.35) is the first item of week 2, together with
   `cauchy-disc-except` (one exceptional point of continuity, the same proof as `cauchy-convex-sub` on
   `cauchy-convex-closed-except`), which 3.7's proof uses.
6. **Kit candidate, named by both agents**: a tactic computing `IS-DIFF-ON` of an explicit CC
   expression (+, *, recip, exp, log, compositions) together with its derivative value; CA-1's
   derivative bookkeeping for c + r exp(it) was its grind, and `winding-circle-inside` (item 4,
   not done) is estimated at 300-400 driver lines without it.
7. **Not done**: `winding-circle-inside` (route proposed: the log of 1 - (z-c)/(w-c) on the exterior
   of a disc); the distance from a point to a compact trace (turns Lemma 3.33 into "analytic off the
   trace"); the radius at least r; the derivative formula 3.40 / 3.42; Prop 3.47 in the notes' form.

Measure, from M-1:

10. **Infima load late** (`einf-*` in limsup-tests, 3380): the measure file's floor.
11. **Countable intersection has no binder**: continuity from above is written through complements.

## 27-A: the notes' 2.14 and the ratio test (10 theorems, four files)

**`cps-taylor-coefficients`** (`theorem-library/cps-taylor-coefficients.scm`) is Proposition 2.14
of complex-analysis.pdf for a function GIVEN as a power series at 0, which is where the notes'
proof lands after its translation ("we may assume a = 0"). The tree has no iterated derivative
over CC (`NTH-DERIV` is RR -> RR only) and no `IS-ANALYTIC`, so the statement reads f^(k) as
F_k, the sum function of the k-th derived series, whose coefficients (n + k)!/n! a_{n+k} are
given by their values, as `cps-radius-derived` gives the first derived sequence. For every real r
with 0 < r < R: F_k is holomorphic on B(0, r); DERIV-ON(F_k) = F_{k+1} at every point of the
ball; F_k(0) = k! a_k. **`cps-coefficient-formula`** reads the last clause as a_k = F_k(0)/k!,
the notes' "f(z) = sum f^(k)(0)/k! z^k" coefficient by coefficient. The three bricks:
`cps-kth-derived-step` (the (k+1)-th sequence is the derived sequence of the k-th: the
arithmetic (n+k+1)!/n! = (n+1) (n+1+k)!/(n+1)!), `cps-kth-derived-radius` (induction on k
through `cps-radius-derived`; k = 0 by function extensionality), `cps-sum-at-zero` (a power
series at 0 sums to its constant term).

**`ratio-test-converges`** (`theorem-library/ratio-test-proof.scm`) is the support of
`structure-library/power-series.scm` proven literally (the band printed "re-installing the same
statement"), from `ratio-test-converges-limsup` (batch 25-A, the notes' 2.15 first half) through
one new bridge, `elimsup-eventually-le` (a sequence bounded by r from some index on has lim sup at
most r); x = 0 is a separate case, since the lim sup test wants strictly positive terms. Nothing
cited the support; its topic is kept.

**`factorial-in-nn`** (`theorem-library/factorial-in-nn.scm`): the typing n! in NN was a bare
`theory-add-axiom!` in injection.scm with no warrant, found when a first draft of 2.14 typed
FACTORIAL through it and billed `trust: none`. Proven by nn-induction from the two defining
equations; the axiom retired. **`deriv-on-of-is-diff-on`** and **`cc-deriv-on-of-is-diff-on`**
(`theorem-library/deriv-on-read-off.scm`): IS-DIFF-ON(K, U, f, a, L) gives DERIV-ON(K, U, f, a)
= L over a normed field with arbitrarily small nonzero elements, and over CC with that
hypothesis discharged. The 2.14 driver did this read-off inline three times (`iota-d`,
`diff-on-unique`, `cc-nf-small-elements`); every future "f' = g over CC" is now one citation.

# Batch 23 (2026-09-23): the integral along a road exists

Dieudonne IX.6, after (8.7.2): the integral of a continuous f along a road gamma is the integral
over [a, b] of f(gamma(t)) gamma'(t), which exists because the integrand is regulated -- a
continuous function of a continuous function is continuous, and the product of a continuous and a
regulated function is regulated. In the tree, `LINE-INT` is defined through `CC-INT` and `PW-INT`
(an IOTA over a primitive), and every law of `CC-INT` and `LINE-INT` carried the two facts "a
primitive of the real part of the integrand exists" and "of the imaginary part" as antecedents.
With (8.7.2) proven (batch 22) those are a theorem, proven once here.

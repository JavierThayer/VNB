## 26-A: the notes' 2.10 (20 theorems, `series-domination.scm`, `cc-power-series-deriv.scm`)

`cps-holomorphic-ball`: for every real r with 0 < r < R, the sum function of sum a_k z^k is
holomorphic on B(0, r), the derived series sum (k+1) a_{k+1} z^k has the same radius
(`cps-radius-derived`), and f'(u) is its sum (`cps-diff-on-ball`). The one convention: the
library has no ball of radius +inf, so the statement is made on B(0, r) for each such r, which
covers B(0, R) for finite R and the plane for R = +inf. The three lemmas the previous batch named
came first: domination by a convergent non-negative series from some index on
(`series-eventually-dominated-converges`, with `series-shift-converges-iff`), the power estimates
(first and second order: r |z^{k+1} - u^{k+1} - (k+1) u^k (z - u)| <= (k+1)^2 r^k |z - u|^2 for
|z|, |u| <= r), and linearity of the complex series sum (`cc-series-limit-linear`). Two departures
from the notes' proof: no telescoping identity (a one-step recurrence Q_{k+1} = z Q_k +
(k+1) u^k (z - u)^2 gives the estimate by induction and replaces the double sum), and no
lim n^{1/n} = 1 (absent from the tree; a radius-comparison lemma `cps-radius-mono`, an index
shift `cps-shift-conv`, and 2.9 with b_k = k give the radius equality). A general lemma worth
its own name: `cc-diff-on-quadratic`, a bound |f(x) - f(a) - (x - a) L| <= M |x - a|^2 on an open
set gives the derivative L at a.

## 26-B: det is multiplicative (30 theorems, `det-alternating-form.scm`, `det-multiplicative.scm`)

`det-alternating-form`, Hoffman-Kunze 5.3 Theorem 2: a function D of n x n matrices over a
commutative ring that is linear in each row and zero on any matrix with two equal rows
satisfies D(A) = det(A) D(I), for every n including 0; the property is written with formula
builders in the shape of `det-row-linear` and `det-alternating-rows`. The proof is an induction
on n without permutations or signs: first row e_1 by clearing column 1 (D unchanged) and
`bordered-eq-border`, then the induction hypothesis on M -> D(BORDER(1, M)); first row e_j by
right multiplication with the swap matrix F[1, j], on both D and det, so the sign (-1)^{1+j} is
never computed; a general first row one entry at a time by linearity in row 1. Then
`det-multiplicative`, EXACTLY as its support stated it (X -> det(XQ) is such a D, with
D(I) = det Q), the support retired from `structure-library/determinant.scm`, which now carries no
support at all; and the notes' 3.20, `det-mul-invertible`, over a field with an invertible factor.

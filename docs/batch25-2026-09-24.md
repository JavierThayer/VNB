## 25-C: the determinant (43 theorems, `det-rows.scm`, `det-mul.scm`)

Over any commutative ring, from the first-row cofactor definition: `det-row-linear` (linear in
each row), `det-swap-rows` (interchanging two rows negates det), `det-alternating-rows` (two equal
rows give 0) -- NOW A THEOREM, the support retired from `structure-library/determinant.scm`;
the elementary row operations (det(HA) = c det A, det(GA) = det A, det(FA) = -det A) and
det(EA) = det E det A for each elementary E; `det-expand-row`, expansion along ANY row with the
sign (-1)^{i+j}. Hoffman-Kunze define det along a column, which makes alternation in the rows a
two-term cancellation; with the library's first-row definition the rows needed a second level of
expansion (rows 1 and 2 equal: a double sum cancelling in pairs, no division by 2, so
characteristic 2 is covered).

**Still asserted: `det-multiplicative`.** Not for want of a field: the one missing lemma is
Hoffman-Kunze 5.3 Theorem 2, an alternating n-linear D satisfies D(A) = det(A) D(I), which
proves 3.27 for every commutative ring and 3.20 with it; with linearity and alternation proven it
is about one more file. The column statements and det(A^t) = det(A) wait on a TRANSPOSE, a
definition proposed in the report and not added. The tree also has no theorem that an invertible
matrix over a field is a product of elementary matrices.

## 5. Findings

Errors in the sources:
1. **The notes' estimate (96) is wrong as written** (CA-6): it bounds the tail at infinity by
   M e^{-R} from e^{-t} t^{s-1} <= M, which is not an integrable majorant. The proof uses
   (s - 1) log t <= A (log 2A - 1) + t/2, hence |integrand| <= C e^{-t/2}.
2. **The notes' proof of Prop. 2.28 (dominated convergence) has a gap** (M-3): it uses
   integral(h_k) <= integral(f_k) <= integral(f), but f_k <= f is not a hypothesis. The upper bound
   needs a second Fatou on g - f_k, as in Rudin 1.34; that is the proof given.
3. **The strand-space paper, sections 2.1 to 2.4** (S-1, as the user warned): inv is described by
   a model, not an axiom (the law taken: inv(inv k) = k); T and K disjoint is required but nothing
   needs it; "the smallest relation" of Definition 2.11 needs A to be a set, hence T and K sets and
   replacement for the ternary product; Proposition 2.12 is not "immediate" -- it needs an unstated
   inversion lemma (a subterm of {g}K is {g}K or a subterm of g) and its real hypothesis is that the
   two encryptions differ, not that the keys do; the paper never says the subterm relation is a
   partial order, and antisymmetry needs the rank; WIDTH (2.10) is not definable yet, for want of
   recursion over the free algebra (the tree recurses over NN only); the key as a message and the
   key used to encrypt are conflated in the paper since K is a subset of A there.

Gaps in the tree:
4. No product rule for roads or CC-valued primitives: `road-product-constant` does it
   coordinatewise (120 lines); a `road-product` brick and a CC-valued `has-deriv-at` product law
   would dissolve it.
5. No distance from a point to a compact trace, no "a compact subspace is closed": the radius of
   `winding-locally-constant` comes from `aif-trace-bounded` applied to 1/(w - z0).
6. No integration by parts for CC-INT (the recursion of Gamma went through the fundamental theorem).
7. No "component": `winding-zero-unbounded` is stated for a connected set reaching outside a disc.
8. `qq-dense-in-rr`, still asserted, now looks provable from `rr-nn-floor-mul` and `rr-grid-between`.
9. The almost-everywhere vocabulary does not exist; `integral-finite-null`, `integral-le-off-null`
   and `integral-sub` are its seed.
10. No recursion over an inductively defined set (the free algebra): width, and later the penetrator
    bounds, want it.

Lints and kit:
11. **No gate catches two BINDERS of a statement that differ only by case** (CA-6 first proved a
    weaker `cc-int-compare-primitive` with `g` and `G`, one variable to the machine). The define and
    local lints exist; the statement lint does not. To add to the fatal binder audit.
12. `dk-diff!` defects (CA-6): unary minus comes out as (-1) * w in the derivative's user form, so
    the normalisation cannot match and the K-form is landed with a warning; a literal (* -1 ...) factor
    in the body crashes it ("dk-landed: no assumption"); a constant built from abs / LOG / magnitude is
    not typed. Workarounds: pass the derivative explicitly; `dk-name!` the constant. It worked for
    CA-5 (the first field use).
13. `dk-project!` fails on a compound argument (`(IS-SIMPLE-FN omega cA (cf n))`); `dk-fact!` errors
    when the instance is in context (`dk-cite!` does not); `dk-apply!` at the same terms twice errors;
    `keep` followed by `prop` drifts when `keep` reports "already proven" (three agents wrote the same
    guarded helper); `dk-close-if!` reads `keep`'s normal move as drift; `ineq` poisoned by NN-typed
    atoms (CA-6's `gmf-ineq!`, a kit candidate); the `t = t` leaf of an instantiation at an untyped
    application, again. The helpers M-2 and M-3 copied (`ibk-*` -> `icv-*`, the `cif-` and `wnd-`

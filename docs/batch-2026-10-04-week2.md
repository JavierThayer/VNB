## 5. The gaps (what the notes say that the tree cannot yet say)

Complex analysis:
1. **The closed disc.** Every disc statement asks for an open disc of a LARGER radius inside U, not
   for the closed disc D(c, r) inside U. The bridge is a compactness lemma the tree lacks: a closed
   disc inside an open set lies in a slightly larger open disc. `holomorphic-analytic-at` shows
   nothing is lost for analyticity; the statements of 3.7 and 3.8 in the notes' form want the lemma.
2. **No evaluation map POLY(CC) -> FUN(CC, CC).** The FTA is stated on an explicit coefficient
   function; with the map it is one citation on POLY / DEG.
3. **No connectedness** (week 1, gap 1, again): the maximum modulus principle stops at the disc form;
   the open-set form and the constancy statements of 3.4 wait for IS-CONNECTED.
4. **Cost.** `cauchy-integral-formula-disc` alone proves in 340 s and the whole of CA-3 in about
   1850 s: a very large main context with many per-point typing lanes. The exam grows by about half
   an hour tonight. Moving sub-results into lanes is the fix; the file is correct as it stands.
5. **Local uniform convergence** is written as the ball-wise statement of `line-int-of-uniform-limit`,
   equivalent on an open set to "uniformly on compact subsets", which has no predicate.

Measure:
6. **[0, +inf] had no etimes algebra and only abelian-group fibred summation**; both exist now as
   bricks. The ESUP-past-eplus laws (week 1, gaps 8-9) were avoided by two inequalities.
7. **Strict `=` and fibres**: x lies in its own fibre {phi = phi(x)} only when phi(x) is defined;
   `sep-fiber-insert-same` carries the definedness guard.
8. **`mac` of a nested functoid unfolds every layer**: a nested PTWISE-SCALE wants the inner
   function named first (`dk-name!`).

Kit, from all three (the retirement pass and the fixes are owed):
9. **Two kit defects**: `dk-conj-close!` (and the `in-sep!`-style loops) refocus a leaf that
   hash-consing has already grounded, so the closer fires on whatever has the focus; `dk-set-close!`
   on a CARTESIAN recurses and fires a bare `ass` at every level. M-2 worked around both locally.
10. **Helpers for the kit**, from M-2: finite-set induction for a property of a set (`ibk-induct!`),
    capture-free pointwise claims, set equality by extensionality, `dk-abstract!` with a pre-beta
    sethood hook (the kit version fails when lambda values need a sethood fact).
11. **A `--quiet` option on `vnb-probe`** (CA-4): the state dumps are most of a probe's wall time
    (morera.scm 237 s loud, 46 s quiet; the mean-value file over 1800 s loud, 377 s quiet).
12. **The traps, again**: case folding inside one `let*` (CA-3 four times, CA-4 three); a claim equal
    to the focus goal closed in place by `dk-have!` or `keep`, the next bare `ass` on a sibling
    (`dk-ass!` throughout); the `t = t` leaf of an instantiation at an untyped term
    (`metric-limit-unique`, `taylor-coefficients-at`); `dk-cite!` detaching only part of a chain
    without complaint when a guard is missing.

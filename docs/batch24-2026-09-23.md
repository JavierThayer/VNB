## The file: `theorem-library/line-int-laws.scm` (11 theorems, one agent)

The laws of the line integral for a ROAD and a CONTINUOUS f, with no primitive in the
statement, each with exactly the hypotheses of `line-int-exists` (batch 23): `line-int-sum` and
`line-int-scalar` (the notes: (44) is complex linear in f), `line-int-opposite-road` (Dieudonne
9.6.1, unconditional in the primitives), `line-int-adjacent` (split at c in (a, b): the sum of
the integrals along the two restricted roads; the notes' Prop 3.1 in split form and Lemma 3.4),
`line-int-abs-bound` (the notes' (54), the integral form; the length form (53) needs the LENGTH
of a road, which is not defined), `line-int-value` (G1(b) - G1(a) + (G2(b) - G2(a)) i for any
primitives of the coordinates; stated without the road, it is the more general form). Three
bricks that were missing: `road-restrict` (the restriction of a road to [c, d] is a road),
`cc-int-congruence` (CC-INT depends only on the values on [a, b]), and
`regulated-on-magnitude` with its one-sided-limit twins (the modulus of a CC-valued function
with regulated coordinates is regulated -- without it 8.7.2 cannot supply the primitive of |phi|
that the estimate needs).

## What was found in the proofs

* Focus drift when a lane's own leaf closes: a "is the focus grounded" test then fires the
  closer on a sibling. The cure is to remember the leaf a lane started on; a kit `dk-lane` is
  wanted (the third file to hand-roll it).
* The imaginary unit in a term the driver builds is the Scheme number `+i`; `'1i` is a fresh
  symbol and fails as "goal not in context".
* The chain-detaching helper appeared for the third time (`lil-chain!` = 22-B's `pul-chain!`):
  kit item.
* No congruence lemma for a primitive itself; `pw-antiderivative-real-mul` at 1 stands in.

   to be near SOME member of `F`, and its header claimed that `F` lies in `PTS(s)` "by the typing
   of DIST". The typing constrains only the centres that are used; `F` may be the universal
   class, so `CARD F in NN` in TOTALLY-BOUNDED constrained nothing, and `tb-rad-ball-cover` and
   `tb-scale-dense-seq` fail in a model. Repair decided by the user: the clause `F subseteq A`.
   It is also what lets the nets at a given radius be collected (a SEP of `POWER(PTS s)`), which
   the proof of `tb-rad-ball-cover` needs in order to choose a net as a function of the index.
2. OPEN, USER DECISION -- **`span-add-one-superset` and `span-add-one-has-v` are false as stated.**

   module laws of `m`. Model: `VEC = {0, 1}`, `VADD` and `ACT` constant 0, `t = {0, 1}`, `v = 1`;
   then `SPAN-ADD-ONE(m, t, v) = {0}`. *Recommendation: add `(IS-NORMED-VECTOR-SPACE m)`, which
   `span-add-one-submodule` already carries and both citers have in context.*
